Photos

Before photos

When on, each visit starts with a Before photos step so you can capture the pool before any work is done. Turning this off removes that step entirely.

After photos

When on, each visit ends with an After photos step so you can capture the pool once the work is done. Turning this off removes that step entirely.

Require before photos

When on, technicians must take a before photo on every pool, spa, and extra body of water before moving on. Has no effect while the Before photos step is switched off.

Require after photos

When on, technicians must take an after photo before they can submit the service report. Has no effect while the After photos step is switched off.

Require a closed gate photo

When on, technicians must photograph the closed or locked gate on the After photos step before they can submit the service report.

Field app

Voice entry button

Shows a microphone next to the step button. Tap it and speak readings or dosages — "chlorine three, pH seven point four" — and they fill in wherever they belong. It only listens while you're speaking, so there's no constant chiming.

Require a reason for skipped services

When on, skipping a service opens a screen where the technician must explain why, with the option to attach a photo.

Require readings before moving on

When on, every chemical reading must be filled in before a technician can leave the readings step. Filter clean visits are exempt.

Allow technicians to rearrange routes

When on, technicians can drag customers into a different order on their Today list. Turning this off locks the route order for everyone.

Technicians tab

Turn this off if you don't have technicians — it hides the Technicians tab from the menu entirely.
