Settings

More settings will show up on this page as they're added.

Storage

Everything lives in this browser's storage, which browsers cap at roughly 5–10 MB. Photos take up the most room by far. When it fills, nothing new can be saved.

Removes photos from all but the four most recent visits per body of water. Readings, notes and service records are kept in full — only the images go.

Backup & restore

Download a snapshot of everything — customers, technicians, readings, equipment, routes, and settings. Keep it somewhere safe. If data is ever changed or deleted by mistake, restoring a backup puts it all back.

Show pool size details

When on, pool volume (in gallons) can be entered when adding or editing a customer, and shows on their profile right under their technician. When off, the field is hidden entirely.

Photos

Before photos step

When on, each visit in the field apps starts with a Before photos step so techs can capture the pool before any work is done. Turning this off removes that step entirely.

After photos step

When on, each visit in the field apps ends with an After photos step so techs can capture the pool once the work is done. Turning this off removes that step entirely.

Require before photos

When on, technicians must take a before photo on every pool, spa, and extra body of water before moving on. Has no effect while the Before photos step is switched off below.

Require after photos

When on, technicians must take an after photo before they can submit the service report. Has no effect while the After photos step is switched off below.

Require a closed gate photo

When on, technicians must photograph the closed or locked gate on the After photos step before they can submit the service report. Proof the property was left secure.

Field app

Voice entry button

Shows a microphone next to the step button in the field apps. Technicians tap it and speak readings or dosages — "chlorine three, pH seven point four" — and the values fill in wherever they belong. It only listens while they speak.

Require a reason for skipped services

When on, skipping a service opens a screen where the technician must explain why, with the option to attach a photo. The reason is saved with that visit.

Require readings before moving on

When on, technicians must fill in every chemical reading before they can leave the readings step of a visit. Filter clean visits are exempt.

Allow technicians to rearrange routes

When on, technicians can drag customers into a different order on their Today list in the field apps. Turning this off locks the route order for everyone.

Dosage suggestion buttons

When on, dosage fields in the field app show quick-tap buttons with suggested amounts, instead of just a blank number field.
